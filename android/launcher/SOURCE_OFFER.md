## Fold Craft Launcher components

The APK contains SHA-256-pinned runtime files extracted without modification from:

- FCL release: `@FCL_VERSION@`
- Binary release: https://github.com/FCL-Team/FoldCraftLauncher/releases/tag/@FCL_VERSION@
- Corresponding source: https://github.com/FCL-Team/FoldCraftLauncher/tree/@FCL_VERSION@

The extracted paths and their SHA-256 values are declared in `android/launcher/app/build.gradle`.
